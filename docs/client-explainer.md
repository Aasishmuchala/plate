# How we use AI on your project, in plain words

We build your images from the 3D model in 3ds Max and V-Ray. When we use AI, it is a finishing tool inside a fence we draw ourselves. This page says what that fence is.

## Your render stays your render

- **Anything we did not mean to change is your original render, pixel for pixel.** AI may touch only the parts we mark as editable, such as trees, sky, people or water. The building, its glass, its signs and its materials are locked.
- **We never enlarge your render with AI.** If an image needs more detail, we render it again.
- **Every image we deliver carries a record inside the file** of how it was made, so any copy can be traced back to the exact version and settings.

## What a "gate report" means

Every AI-assisted image is checked by measurements, not opinion, and compared with the original render. The checks ask: did anything move? did the building's edges stay where they were? did the colours of the materials stay true? does the fine detail look natural, neither smeared nor over-sharpened? did anything change outside the area we edited?

Each image gets one result:

| result | what it means |
|---|---|
| **Pass** | every check that can block an image was met |
| **Check** | nothing blocking failed, but a softer guide was missed; a person looks at it |
| **Fail** | a blocking check was missed. The image is kept, marked, and **not delivered** unless a person writes down why |

The report shows each measurement next to the limit, and the version of the checks that produced it. If we later improve the checks, old reports stay as they were.

## Where your images go ("custody")

Some AI services run on our own computers. Others are online companies. We grade every route:

| grade | meaning |
|---|---|
| **C0** | stays on our computers; nothing is sent anywhere |
| **C1** | a company that has agreed **in writing** not to keep your image and not to learn from it |
| **C2** | a reseller whose written terms we hold |
| **D** | we do not hold written terms, or the image would sit at a public web address |

Today we do not yet hold written terms from any online service, so we treat every online route as **D**. For projects under a confidentiality agreement, we use only C0 and C1: today that means our own computers only. On an open project, nothing is sent online until the studio has recorded your consent on the project; on a project under a confidentiality agreement, the software itself refuses every route below C1. What leaves our computers is only the picture itself, without names, file details or project information.

## Nothing is spent without a price

Before any online AI run, we see the price, the service and its grade. Nothing is spent until a person has approved it, either that run, or a per-run cap or budget envelope the studio set in advance.

## Marks you may see

- **PREVIS** across a moving picture: a rough preview made by AI, to agree a camera move with you. It is not a measured image and **it is never a deliverable**.
- **CONCEPT, UNMEASURED** across a still: an idea sketch, not checked against the model. It is never delivered as a final image.
- **REVIEW** across a picture in a review bundle: a reduced copy for your comments, not the delivery file.

Questions about any of this are welcome; we would rather you ask than guess.
