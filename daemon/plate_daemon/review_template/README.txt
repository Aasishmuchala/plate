{{TITLE}}
====================

This folder is a review of {{TAKES}} image(s). Nothing here needs to be installed
and nothing is sent anywhere while you look at it.

HOW TO OPEN
  1. Unzip the file (right-click, Extract All on Windows; double-click on a Mac).
  2. Open index.html in any current browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
     It works offline, straight from the folder.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE
  Before: the original image.   After: the finished version.
  - Drag across the picture, or use the Wipe slider, to slide between them.
  - Toggle (T) flips between Before and After. 100% (Z) shows the picture at its
    own pixel size so you can check fine detail. [ and ] move between takes.
  - The coloured tag under each picture is the quality check for that take:
    PASS, FAIL or ADVISORY, with the first measurement that did not pass.

THE PICTURES ARE PROXIES
  They are reduced in size and carry a "{{WATERMARK}}" watermark. They are for
  review only and are not the files that will be delivered.

SENDING YOUR COMMENTS BACK
  Fill in the form on the right: a decision and a note for each take, then an
  overall note. Then use either button:
  - Send by email opens a new message with your comments already written.
    {{REPLY}}
  - Download comments.json saves your comments as a small file. Attach it to
    an email if the first button does not open your mail program, or if your
    notes are long.
  Your notes are also kept in this browser until you clear its data, so closing
  the page does not lose them.
